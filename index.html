A LIFE THAT CHANGED SCIENCE. 1867-1934

MARIE
CURIE.

Marie Curie (1867 to 1934) was a pioneering physicist and chemist known for her groundbreaking research on radioactivity. She was also the first person to receive Nobel Prizes in two different scientific fields, Physics and Chemistry. Her remarkable discoveries transformed modern science and medicine, making her one of history's most influential scientists.
